Common questions

How many free scans do I get? 10 scans a month. A scan only counts when Pinyini finds Chinese in it. Pinyini Pro has unlimited scans.

How do I cancel Pinyini Pro? Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Pinyini.

I subscribed on another iPhone. In Pinyini, open Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Translations aren't showing. The first translation may need to download a language pack from Apple, which needs an internet connection once.
