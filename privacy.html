Pinyini Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Pinyini shows the pinyin, meanings and translations of Chinese text you photograph. It's built to do that on your iPhone, without collecting anything about you. This policy explains what that means in detail.

Your photos and text stay on your device

  • Camera and photos. Pinyini uses the camera, or a photo you choose, only to read the Chinese in it. Text recognition runs on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. Pinyini doesn't upload or keep your photos.
  • Pinyin and meanings come from a dictionary built into the app, so looking up a word never leaves your device.
  • Translations use Apple's Translation framework, which translates on your device. The first time you translate, iOS may download a language pack from Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Clipboard. Pinyini reads your clipboard only when you tap Paste, and writes to it only when you tap Copy.

Pinyini has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. The text you scan is never sent to us.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the app work, Pinyini sends a few anonymous usage signals through TelemetryDeck, for example that onboarding was finished, which script you chose (Simplified or Traditional), or that the subscription screen was shown. These signals:

  • never include the text or photos you scan;
  • use an anonymized, hashed identifier instead of any personal information;
  • aren't used for advertising and aren't linked to you or used to track you across apps or websites.

Subscriptions

Pinyini Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and your billing details; we never see them. We only learn from Apple whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences (such as dark mode, text size and your chosen script) and your count of free scans this month are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Children

Pinyini doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
